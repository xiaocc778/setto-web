Installation Guide

Visual site workflow for professional flooring crews.

Use this quick guide as a practical reference before reading the full latest TDS and checking site conditions.

01 Prepare Subfloor

Confirm the substrate is structurally sound, clean, dry, flat and free from dust, grease or loose residue.

02 Use a 5-6 mm V-Notch Trowel

Apply SETTO Supreme S75 with a 5-6 mm V-notch trowel held at a 45 degree angle for controlled adhesive ridges.

03 Maintain 45 Degree Angle

Hold the trowel at approximately 45 degrees to create consistent adhesive height and transfer.

04 Check Adhesive Transfer

Structural bonding requires minimum 80% adhesive transfer between subfloor and timber flooring.

05 Use Full Coverage When Required

100% substrate coverage is required for full moisture and acoustic performance.

FAQs

Common installation questions.

Does the flooring type change the usage estimate?

Current estimates use the latest TDS coverage basis. Flooring type is used as project context for technical support and quoting.

When should 100% coverage be used?

Use 100% substrate coverage when full moisture and acoustic performance is required, subject to site conditions and latest TDS guidance.
