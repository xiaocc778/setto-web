Installation Workflow

Typical S75 Site Sequence

01

Prepare the Subfloor

Concrete or screed must be structurally sound, flat, dust-free, and checked for moisture before laying timber.

02

Apply with 5-6 mm V-notch

Spread S75 evenly and keep the adhesive bed open for 20 to 40 minutes depending on site temperature and humidity.

03

Lay for Full Transfer

Press boards firmly to achieve minimum 80% transfer, or 100% coverage when barrier and acoustic performance are required.
