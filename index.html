How to Use

Installation Workflow

01

Prepare Subfloor

Concrete must be clean, dry, flat within 3 mm over 3 m, and free from dust, oils, sealers, and old adhesive residue.

02

Apply Adhesive

Use a 5-6 mm V-notch trowel held at 45 degrees for full, even coverage.

03

Lay and Press Boards

Place flooring into the wet adhesive bed and achieve 100% coverage for full moisture and acoustic performance.
