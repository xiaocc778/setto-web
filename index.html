Installation

Simple Professional Installation

01

Prepare Subfloor

Ensure the substrate is structurally sound, clean, dry, flat and free from dust, grease and residual adhesives.

02

Apply Adhesive

Use a 5–6 mm V-notch trowel at a 45° angle for even application.

03

Install Flooring

Place timber flooring into the wet adhesive bed according to manufacturer guidelines.

04

Cure & Finish

Allow foot traffic after 24 hours and light sanding after 24 hours where suitable.
