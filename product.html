Product Description

A single-component MS polymer system for professional timber flooring installation.

SETTO Supreme S75 is designed for solid timber flooring, engineered timber flooring, multi-layer parquet systems, end-grain wood blocks, bamboo flooring, cork flooring systems, and dimensionally sensitive or exotic timber species.
