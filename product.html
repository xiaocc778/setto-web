Description

Setto Supreme S75 is a premium, single-component, moisture-curing MS hybrid adhesive developed for professional timber flooring installations.

Advantages

  • Solvent-free, isocyanate-free and extremely low VOC.
  • Strong, permanently elastic and durable bond.
  • Moisture barrier and acoustic support in one full-coverage application.
  • Compatible with radiant underfloor heating systems.

Technical Data

  • Open Time: 20–40 minutes.
  • Skin Formation: approx. 30–60 minutes.
  • Full Cure Rate: approx. 3 mm / 24 h.
  • Full-Coverage Consumption: 700–1000 g/m².

Application Areas

Suitable for solid timber flooring, engineered timber flooring, multi-layer parquet systems, end-grain wood blocks, bamboo flooring, cork systems and dimensionally sensitive timber species.

Subfloor Preparation

Concrete must be structurally sound, clean, dry, flat and free from dust, grease, oils, sealers, curing compounds and residual adhesives.

Installation Procedure

Apply evenly using a 5–6 mm V-notch trowel held at a 45° angle. Place flooring elements into the wet adhesive bed and maintain manufacturer-specified expansion gaps.

Packaging & Storage

Available in 10 L / 15 kg pails and 590 ml sausages. Shelf life is 12 months in original unopened packaging when stored at 5–25°C.

Health & Safety

Not classified as hazardous under GHS criteria. May cause mild skin or eye irritation in sensitive individuals. Refer to the latest SDS for full safety and disposal details.
