Core Advantages

Why Flooring Teams Choose S75

Permanent Elastic Bond

Strong adhesion with dimensional stability helps timber layouts stay aligned while retaining long-term flexibility.

Low Odor, Low VOC

Solvent-free and isocyanate-free chemistry is suited to interior renovation environments and installer comfort.

Moisture Barrier Performance

With full 100% coverage, the cured layer forms a continuous moisture-resistant film beneath the flooring system.

Acoustic Damping Benefit

One-step installation supports impact sound reduction without needing a separate acoustic underlay layer.
