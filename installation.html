Installation

Professional S75 Installation

A controlled process helps SETTO Supreme S75 deliver permanent elastic bonding, superior moisture barrier support and installation comfort in a professional MS polymer timber flooring adhesive system.

Procedure

Apply, transfer, cure, and finish with confidence.

Use a 5-6 mm V-notch trowel held at a 45 degree angle. Minimum 80% adhesive transfer is required for structural bonding. 100% coverage is recommended for full moisture barrier support and foot-traffic comfort.

01

Prepare Subfloor

Ensure the substrate is structurally sound, clean, dry, flat and free from dust, grease and residual adhesives.

02

Apply Adhesive

Hold the V-notch trowel at 45 degrees and maintain an even adhesive bed during application.

03

Install Flooring

Place timber flooring elements into the wet adhesive bed in accordance with manufacturer guidance.

04

Cure & Finish

Light foot traffic and light sanding are possible after approximately 24 hours, subject to site conditions.
