Installation

Professional S75 Installation

A controlled process helps SETTO Supreme S75 deliver permanent elastic bonding, superior moisture barrier support and installation comfort in a professional MS polymer timber flooring adhesive system.

Procedure

Apply, transfer, cure, and finish with confidence.

Use a 5-6 mm V-notch trowel held at a 45 degree angle. Aim for minimum 80% transfer for structural bonding, or 100% coverage for full moisture and acoustic performance.

5-6 mm V-notch 45 degree angle 80% transfer 100% coverage option
01

Prepare Subfloor

Clean, dry, flat and structurally sound.

02

Apply Adhesive

Spread even ridges at a 45 degree angle.

03

Install Flooring

Place boards into the wet adhesive bed.

04

Cure & Finish

Allow curing before light traffic or sanding.
