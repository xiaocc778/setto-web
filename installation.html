Professional S75 Installation

A controlled process helps SETTO Supreme S75 deliver adhesive bonding, moisture protection and acoustic reduction in one 3-in-1 MS polymer timber flooring adhesive system.

01

Prepare Subfloor

Ensure the substrate is structurally sound, clean, dry, flat and free from dust, grease and residual adhesives.

02

Apply Adhesive

Use a 5-6 mm V-notch trowel held at a 45° angle. Maintain an even adhesive bed during application.

03

Install Flooring

Place timber flooring into the wet adhesive bed. Minimum 80% adhesive transfer is required for structural bonding, and 100% coverage is recommended for full moisture and acoustic performance.

04

Cure & Finish

Light foot traffic and light sanding are possible after approximately 24 hours, subject to site conditions.
