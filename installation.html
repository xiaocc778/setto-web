Setto Supreme S75 is designed for fast, controlled installation with stable transfer across herringbone, parquet, and engineered timber projects.

Recommended Process

3 Core Installation Steps

01

Prepare Subfloor

Check flatness, remove dust and residue, and confirm the substrate is suitable before opening the pail.

02

Apply Adhesive Bed

Spread evenly at a 45 deg angle using a 5-6 mm V-notch trowel for full and consistent ridge formation.

03

Lay and Weight Boards

Install within open time, maintain expansion gaps, and apply pressure to reach the transfer target.
