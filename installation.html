Professional S75 Installation

A controlled process helps SETTO Supreme S75 deliver permanent elastic bonding, superior moisture barrier support and installation comfort in a professional MS polymer timber flooring adhesive system.

The Professional Process

Five steps to a perfect install

01

Prepare Subfloor

Ensure the subfloor is clean, dry, flat and structurally sound. Remove dust, oils and loose contaminants.

02

Apply Adhesive

Use a suitable 5-6 mm V-notch trowel to apply SETTO Supreme S75 evenly.

03

Maintain 45 Degree Angle

Hold the trowel at a consistent 45 degree angle to achieve uniform ridges and optimal transfer.

04

Check Transfer

Ensure adequate adhesive transfer to the back of the timber for maximum bond performance.

05

Cure & Finish

Allow adhesive to cure within the recommended time before foot traffic and sanding if required.
