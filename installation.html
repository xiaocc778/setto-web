Standard Process

Installation Sequence

01

Prepare Subfloor

Concrete must be structurally sound, clean, dry, flat, and free from dust, grease, oils, sealers, curing compounds, and residual adhesives.

02

Apply Adhesive

Apply evenly with a 5-6 mm V-notch trowel held at a 45 degree angle.

03

Lay and Press Flooring

Place flooring into the wet adhesive bed, maintain expansion gaps, and apply firm pressure where needed.

Clean-up

Wet Adhesive Cleans Fastest

Wipe tools immediately with a dry cloth, then clean with acetone or mineral spirits if needed. Test solvents before using on prefinished timber.

Remove mechanically with a scraper, putty knife, oscillating tool, or dedicated cured MS polymer remover.

Wear gloves, ensure ventilation when using solvents, and refer to the latest SDS for disposal details.
