Refund Policy

How SETTO Australia reviews return and refund requests for trade and project supply orders.

Last updated: May 2026

Return Review

Return or refund requests are reviewed based on order details, supply conditions, product condition and applicable Australian consumer law. Please contact us promptly with invoice details and supporting information.

Unopened Goods

Where accepted, returned goods should be unopened, unused, in original packaging and stored in accordance with product requirements. Freight or handling costs may apply depending on the circumstances.

Opened or Misused Product

Products that have been opened, incorrectly stored, contaminated, used on site or applied outside relevant guidance are not automatically eligible for refund.

Contact

For return or refund enquiries, contact info@setto.com.au with your order details.
