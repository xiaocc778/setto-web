Privacy Policy

How SETTO Australia handles business contact information submitted through enquiries, trade account requests and technical support communication.

Last updated: May 2026

Information We Collect

We may collect business contact details such as your name, company, phone number, email address, location, ABN or trade licence information when you submit an enquiry, request samples, apply for trade pricing or contact SETTO Australia for technical support.

How We Use Information

Information is used to respond to quote requests, arrange samples, support trade account enquiries, provide product information, coordinate supply discussions and communicate about SETTO Supreme S75 projects.

Sharing & Storage

We do not sell business contact information. Information may be shared with internal staff, importers, distributors or logistics partners where reasonably required to respond to your enquiry or coordinate project supply.

Contact

For privacy questions or correction requests, contact info@setto.com.au.
