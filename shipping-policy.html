Shipping Policy

Shipping and delivery information for SETTO Supreme S75 trade, distributor and project supply enquiries.

Last updated: May 2026

Project Supply Basis

SETTO Australia supports trade, distributor and project supply enquiries. Shipping arrangements are generally confirmed as part of the quotation process rather than through an automated online checkout.

Freight & Delivery Timing

Freight cost, lead time, pallet delivery options and site delivery requirements depend on stock availability, order quantity, delivery address and carrier availability. These details should be confirmed in writing before purchase.

Receiving Orders

Please inspect delivered goods promptly and report any visible freight damage, shortage or delivery issue as soon as practical with supporting photos and order details.
