File Delivery Guidelines

  • Please provide original high-resolution files, not compressed chat app images.
  • PNG, JPG, TIFF, PSD, AI, and PDF files are acceptable.
  • Product images should ideally include transparent or pure white background versions.
  • Scene photos should be landscape, cleanly lit, and show product packaging text clearly where possible.
  • Suggested naming examples: S75-pail-front.png, S75-pail-45degree.jpg, S75-installation-trowel.jpg, S75-TDS-final.pdf.

Post-Delivery Work

After receiving the assets, the design team will crop images for each page, unify color tone, compress web-ready files, export WebP/PNG versions, optimize alt text, and replace all placeholder or generated images across the website.
